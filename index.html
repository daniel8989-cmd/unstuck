Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Claude to use this?

Yes — UNSTUCK is used with Claude. You copy the Master Prompt or one of the 7 ready-to-use prompts into Claude and work through your task step by step.

Is this therapy or mental-health treatment?

No. UNSTUCK is a productivity and task-organization tool. It's designed to help you break down overwhelming tasks and find a clear next action — it does not diagnose or treat conditions like ADHD, anxiety, or depression.

What if I don't understand my assignment?

There's a dedicated "I Don't Understand" prompt in the library that helps you paste in your instructions and figure out exactly what's being asked before you try to complete it.

Will this help with exams and job applications too?

Yes. The prompt library includes dedicated Exam Rescue and CV & Job Application prompts, alongside the general assignment and procrastination tools.

What format do I receive it in?

UNSTUCK is delivered as a single digital PDF containing the Quick-Start Guide, Master Prompt, 7 Prompt Library, and 7-Day Challenge. It's an instant download — no physical item is shipped.
